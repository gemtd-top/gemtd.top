About Gem TD+

This guide is designed specifically for Gem TD+, the popular tower defense game based on the classic Warcraft III custom map.

How to Use This Helper:
  • Click on any gem to mark it as acquired (it will highlight in green)
  • Use the information buttons (ⓘ) to view detailed tower stats
  • Reference the maze design shown earlier for optimal tower placement
Strategy Tips:
  • Premium Targets: Focus on building Dark Emerald, Yellow Sapphire, and Ancient Slate towers
  • Ancient Slates: Place three of these close together near your center Marked RED on the Maze image
  • Early Game: Blood Stone is most effective when acquired early
  • Economy: Jade is primarily useful for its gold-generating upgrade

Your tower configuration will ultimately depend on which gems you're able to obtain during your game. Good luck defending!
